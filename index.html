Desktop: press Space or ↑ to start and jump over obstacles; hold ↓ or S to crawl under hanging obstacles. Touch: tap Jump to start and hop over obstacles; hold Crawl to go under hanging obstacles.

How to play

You run automatically. Your only job is to read what's coming and pick the right move.

  • Jump over hydrants, cones and doghouses. You can only jump while on the ground, so time it.
  • Crawl under hanging obstacles such as park benches, road barriers, laundry and low tree branches. Hold the crawl button or key to stay low; a crawl always lasts a moment, and won't end while you're still under one.
  • The game speeds up as you go, and new obstacle types appear as your score grows.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser only. It isn't verified or ranked against other players.

On a phone, you can add Runner to your home screen and play it offline.
