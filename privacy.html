Runner

Privacy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Runner is a simple game that runs in your browser. This page explains, in plain terms, what it stores and what it doesn't.

What stays on your device

  • Your best score is saved in your browser's local storage so it's there next time. It never leaves your device.
  • If you install the game or play offline, your browser keeps a copy of the game's files in a cache on your device.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or passwords.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No advertising and no advertising cookies.
  • No scores, names, or other gameplay data are sent to a server.

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, the details you type (your message and the email address you choose to give) are sent to a third-party form service so they can be delivered to the site owner. They are used only to reply to you.

Hosting

Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and the pages requested) for security and operations.

Clearing your data

Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove your saved best score and cached files.

Changes

If this changes, for example if ads or analytics are ever added, this page will be updated first, along with any consent options required.
